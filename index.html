Practice that looks after something

Your child chooses an egg and raises the pet inside it. The only thing that makes that pet grow is answering questions: the times tables first, then division and missing numbers.

Feeding, washing, tickling and tucking in at bedtime keep the pet happy. Practice is what helps it grow up.

Made to be handed over

Times Table Star is designed to be something you can give a child without a second thought.

  • No advertsNone at all, and no ad networks or advertising identifiers.
  • No accountsNothing to sign up for. A player is simply a name your child types, and a nickname is fine.
  • No tracking and no data collectionNo analytics and no crash reporting services. We run no servers, so your child's information has nowhere to go, unless you switch on the optional AI chat described below.
  • Progress stays with your familyEverything is kept on the device and backed up to your own private iCloud, which we cannot read. It follows your child to a new iPhone or iPad on the same Apple Account.
  • The AI chat is off unless you turn it onAn optional feature lets the pet talk. It is switched off by default, sits behind a parental gate, and uses your own AI provider key, so conversations run on your account. You can read or erase them, or switch the feature off, at any time.
  • What is earned is keptNothing your child earns by practising is ever taken away or put behind a price.

This website follows the same rules: no cookies, no analytics and no third party scripts. Read the full privacy policy.
