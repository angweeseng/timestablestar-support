TIMES TABLE STAR: FACT SHEET

Name:             Times Table Star
Developer:        Primituz
Based in:         Singapore
First released:   22 April 2026
Current version:  2.1
Platforms:        iPhone and iPad
Age rating:       4+
App Store:        https://apps.apple.com/app/id6762815924
Website:          https://angweeseng.github.io/timestablestar-support/

PRICE
Free to download. Times tables practice is free forever.
One optional, one-time in-app purchase, "Everything", adds the other ten
pets and the Playground they share, Division and Missing Number, ten more
rooms with seasonal decorations, and the full toy box. No subscription.

WHAT IT IS
A maths practice game for children. A child chooses an egg and raises the
pet inside it, and the only thing that makes the pet grow is answering
questions: times tables first, then division and missing numbers.

KEY FEATURES
* Eleven pets: a star, a kitten, an axolotl, a Siamese cat, a puppy, an owl,
  a tortoise, a dragon, a capybara, a rabbit and a fox
* Pets grow through stages (Egg, Baby, Kid, Whizz, Genius) and further ranks
* Six tricks, learned as the pet's IQ rises
* Race a friend on two devices side by side: no internet, no accounts, and
  no names or messages sent between devices
* Five themes with three rooms each, plus seasonal decorations
* A Daily Challenge, the same for everyone each day
* A progress panel for parents showing tricky and mastered facts
* Separate players for brothers and sisters

PRIVACY
No adverts, no accounts, no tracking, no analytics and no data collection.
Progress stays on the device and in the family's own iCloud. The optional AI
chat is off by default, sits behind a parental gate, and uses the parent's
own AI provider key.
