Frequently asked questions

How do I add another child?

Open the Quiz tab and tap Switch player. Choose New player to create a separate profile. Each player keeps their own pets, stars, streaks and level progress. Best scores are shared, so the family can compete on one board.

My pet has not hatched yet

Eggs hatch after three finished quiz rounds. Open the Quiz tab, pick a level and play full rounds of ten questions.

How do I get another pet?

Raise your newest pet to Whizz, which happens at 60 IQ. IQ only comes from playing quizzes. With the optional Everything unlock, a new egg then appears on the Pet Pals shelf.

I bought Everything. How do I restore it on another device?

Tap any padlock, for example a locked room in Settings, Room, and choose Restore Purchases on the card that opens. Make sure the device is signed in with the same Apple Account that made the purchase.

Where is my progress saved? What if I get a new phone?

Everything is saved on the device and backed up automatically to your own iCloud. On a new device signed into the same Apple Account, progress usually appears by itself. If it does not, go to Settings, iCloud Backup, Restore from iCloud.

I cannot hear any sounds

Check the speaker button at the top right of the app is not muted, and that your device volume is up. Game sounds play even when the silent switch is on.

What is the AI companion, and how do I turn it on or off?

It is an optional feature that lets an older pet chat aloud with your child. It is switched off by default. To use it, a parent goes to Settings, AI Brain, passes the parental gate, and adds their own API key from an AI provider they already use. You can read or erase everything the pet remembers, view recent conversations, and switch the feature off completely at any time from the same screen.

How do I stop the daily reminder?

Go to Settings, AI Brain, pass the parental gate, and turn the Daily reminder switch off. It is off unless a parent turns it on.

My child keeps losing hearts and restarting

Each attempt gives three hearts, and a wrong answer or a timeout costs one. If a level is too hard, tap a lower level on the Quiz screen: earlier levels use smaller tables and allow more time per question.

How do I reset everything?

Use Switch player to start a fresh profile. To remove all data entirely, delete the app from your device. Note that iCloud may restore it if you reinstall; email us if you would like help clearing it.

Privacy

Times Table Star collects no personal data. Everything your child creates stays on your device and in your own iCloud. There are no adverts and no tracking. There is one optional in-app purchase, Everything, which is handled entirely by the App Store.
