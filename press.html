About the app

Times Table Star turns maths practice into looking after a pet. A child chooses an egg and raises the pet inside it, and the only thing that makes it grow is answering questions: times tables first, then division and missing numbers. It grows from Egg to Baby, Kid, Whizz and Genius, learns tricks along the way, and lives in rooms the child chooses.

It is built with privacy as the default. There are no adverts, no accounts, no analytics and no data collection. Progress stays on the device and in the family's own iCloud.

Key features

  • Eleven pets to raise: a star, a kitten, an axolotl, a Siamese cat, a puppy, an owl, a tortoise, a dragon, a capybara, a rabbit and a fox
  • Pets grow through stages (Egg, Baby, Kid, Whizz, Genius) and further ranks, only through answering questions
  • Six tricks, learned as the pet's IQ rises
  • Race a friend on two devices side by side, with no internet and no accounts, and no names or messages sent between devices
  • Five themes with three rooms each, and seasonal decorations
  • A Daily Challenge, the same for everyone each day
  • Times tables, division and missing number practice
  • A progress panel for parents showing tricky and mastered facts
  • Separate players for brothers and sisters
